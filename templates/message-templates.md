# Message templates

Fill the `[brackets]`, keep it short, one ask per message.

## Warm intro
> Hey [name] — working on something this week that [outcome they care about].
> Took me [time] to build, it already [does X]. Worth 5 min? [link]

## "Saw you struggling with X"
> [name], saw you [signal — post / comment / known pain] around [X].
> Built a thing that handles exactly that: [one line]. Want me to show you on [yours]? [link]

## Pre-order / payment ask
> Here's the [demo/result]. If it does [outcome] for you, I'm taking [N] early users at [€price].
> Locks in [benefit]. Pay here, live in [timeframe]: [payment link]

## Follow-up (no reply, +1 day)
> Bumping this — still happy to set it up for you. Yes / not now / later?

## Close (soft yes → real money)
> Great — sending the link now so you're locked in at [€price]: [payment link].
> Anything blocking you from doing it in the next 10 min?
