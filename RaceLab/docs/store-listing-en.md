## Long description

**The Strava of RC — but prescriptive.**

40 tunable parameters, one question: what do I change? RaceLab answers it.

Log a run in under 10 seconds, trackside, offline. Attach your setup, your feeling, your transponder laps. Then ask your race engineer: you get **one ranked, reversible change** — with the why, never a pile of guesses.

**Why it's different**
- 🔧 **On-device AI race engineer.** 100% deterministic, zero API calls on the advice path. Works in the pits with no Wi-Fi.
- 🎯 **Grounded or silent.** Every tip cites a rule — or it tells you to keep what you've got.
- 🚗 **237 chassis, every discipline.** Off-road, on-road, carpet, drift, oval, crawler — grounded advice tuned to your car's class.
- 📋 **Immutable setup sheets.** See exactly what changed vs the kit, version by version.
- 🗺️ **Tracks & conditions.** Turn by turn, surfaces, weather, lap records.
- 📊 **Compare two runs** side by side: setup vs conditions vs tyres.
- 🏁 **Time-based race plan.** Pit windows in minutes, not laps.

Nitro or electric, off-road or on-road — the sheet adapts to your car.

**The setup engineer is free, forever.** 15-day free premium trial (race planner, pit strategy, auto-weather, Speedhive import, full history, export). Then **€4.99/month or €39.99/year, billed through Google Play**. Cancel anytime from the Play Store.

Made by racers, for the pit lane. 🏁
