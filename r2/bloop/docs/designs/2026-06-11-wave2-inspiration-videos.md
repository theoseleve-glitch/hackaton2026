## How these are used in the app

- **Card background** = the official platform thumbnail (YouTube: `img.youtube.com` / oEmbed `thumbnail_url`; TikTok: oEmbed) with a visible **creator credit**.
- **Tap** = deep link out to the original video on its platform (official channel URL only).
- Original videos are **NEVER embedded, downloaded, re-hosted, or copied into the app bundle**. Thumbnail + credit + link-out only, removed on any takedown request, never implying creator endorsement of Bloop.
- All URLs below were verified on 2026-06-11 via the official YouTube/TikTok oEmbed endpoints (HTTP 200, title + channel matched). One link was downgraded to a `SEARCH:` placeholder because the only found copy was a re-upload (see cinquante-cinquante notes).

## Per-game notes

### double-jeu
**Why it matches.** Odd Man Out IS this exact mechanic: 6 people share one identity, 1 secret impostor blends in, the group discusses and votes someone out each round — the same discuss-then-vote loop as double-jeu, with the instantly recognizable circle-of-chairs thumbnail.
**Rights.** Official YouTube thumbnail with creator credit linking out is standard low-risk practice (YouTube oEmbed); use `img.youtube.com` rather than re-cropped frames. Jubilee is a media company with formal TV distribution — avoid implying endorsement or using their logo/branding beyond the thumbnail itself.
**Verification.** Primary + all alternates confirmed via oEmbed. Note: "Beta Squad Guess The Imposter ft Deji" is hosted on **More Kenny** (Beta Squad member Kenny's channel), not the main Beta Squad channel — credit More Kenny.

### jeu-de-la-note
**Why it matches.** The viral TikTok "Wavelength / Number Game" format itself (Rolling Stone: "the party game taking over TikTok"): one player guesses a secret number 1-10 while others answer steering questions — the exact jeu-de-la-note loop. Jason Coffee is the biggest named creator playing it; his caption literally describes the mechanic.
**Rights.** Thumbnail + credit via official oEmbed is low-risk, but TikTok clips get deleted/region-locked (keep a fallback), and do NOT reproduce CMYK's trademarked Wavelength dial/board artwork on the card — reference the creator's video, not the board game's trade dress.
**Verification.** All three TikTok videos confirmed live via TikTok oEmbed (Jason Coffee, Highland Bros, Basse And Go) plus the Smosh YouTube alternate.

### cinquante-cinquante
**Why it matches.** The definitive creator take on "we surveyed 100 people — guess what the crowd answered": Beta Squad + Yung Filly guess how a polled crowd responded, exactly cinquante-cinquante's loop. Massive, instantly recognizable format; no comparably iconic French crowd-poll video surfaced.
**Rights.** Thumbnail + credit + link-out to Beta Squad is low risk, BUT "Family Feud" is a Fremantle TV property — never reuse the Family Feud logo or Steve Harvey imagery in card art, only Beta Squad's own thumbnail with credit.
**Verification.** Primary and Sidemen alternate confirmed. The Jubilee "Spectrum" alternate URL turned out to be a **re-upload by "Papa Gut Archive"**, not Jubilee's channel — downgraded to a `SEARCH:` placeholder; find the official Jubilee upload before use.

### devine-la-question
**Why it matches.** Exact mechanic match: a Board AF episode where the Smosh cast is given only the answer and must work backwards to guess the question — the precise reverse-question loop of devine-la-question.
**Rights.** Thumbnail + credit + link-out is standard low-risk; the Smosh thumbnail likely shows Big Potato's commercial board game packaging (third-party trademark), so prefer a frame of the cast over the box art. No confirmed French-creator video of this exact mechanic was found.
**Verification.** Primary confirmed. GMM alternate is actually on the **Good Mythical MORE** companion channel (credit accordingly). The Beta Squad alternate remains a `SEARCH:` placeholder (URL never verified).

### devine-l-action
**Why it matches.** The Sidemen act out secret prompts using mime only while the guesser deduces — the exact "everyone knows except you, helped only through a constrained channel" loop, from a hugely recognizable anglophone crew.
**Rights.** Thumbnail + credit + link-out is low-risk, but Sidemen are brand-protective — use YouTube's official thumbnail via embed/oEmbed or a stylized recreation, and avoid the NSFW-titled variant in-app.
**Verification.** All confirmed. The "unknown Shorts creator" of the reverse-charades Short is **Courtney & Alex** — credit them by name.

### devine-l-objet
**Why it matches.** "What's in the Box" is the definitive viral format for identifying a secret object via constrained clues — exactly the devine-l-objet loop. Collins Key's version is the format's single biggest creator video with an instantly recognizable thumbnail. (The literal "hot/cold hunt" variant has no single iconic creator video — this box format is the recognizable stand-in.)
**Rights.** Thumbnail + credit + link-out is generally tolerated for independent creators (Collins Key, Squeezie, Mastu), but avoid the Fallon/NBC option as anything beyond a text credit — NBCUniversal aggressively enforces even still-frame reuse; never embed or re-host any clip, only deep-link.
**Verification.** All confirmed. Correction: "Y'A QUI DANS LA BOÎTE ? #2" is on **Mastu**'s channel (avec Joyca et Maxime Biaggi), not Mcfly et Carlito — credit Mastu.

### quizz-culture-ge
**Why it matches.** The definitive French "culture générale quiz with a party twist": Quipo Quiz questions where a wrong answer forces calling a random phone contact — same DNA as Bloop's GE quiz with bluff/closest-number twists. 28M+ views, France's two biggest creator brands, national-meme catchphrase.
**Rights.** Thumbnail + credit is low risk, but the thumbnail shows McFly, Carlito and Squeezie's faces — French droit à l'image is strict: keep it clearly editorial/credited, never imply endorsement; avoid reproducing the Quipo Quiz logo (Télé-Québec trademark); Fibbage is a Jackbox trademark, never name the game after it.
**Verification.** Primary and Sidemen alternate confirmed. The Squeezie "Imposteur" alternate remains a `SEARCH:` placeholder per the source data (the same video is verified at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J1Z1A46FknM under double-jeu — reuse that URL).

### double-le-mouvement
**Why it matches.** The cult French "DOUBLAGE" improv series: silent footage live-dubbed with improvised voices and sound effects — exactly the game's mechanic, with a player performing the silent movement instead of the screen. Episode 1 (Harry Potter) is the iconic origin of the format.
**Rights.** Thumbnail + credit + link-out is standard, but crop the Sofyan thumbnail to the creators (the videos contain Warner Bros. / Universal footage), and avoid Whose Line imagery in-app since it is network-owned TV content (CW/Hat Trick).
**Verification.** All four URLs confirmed (Sofyan x2, Bad Lip Reading, Whose Line official channel).

### pires-potes
**Why it matches.** Exact same mechanic: the seven Sidemen vote/point at who in the group is most likely to X, with roasts when answers clash — the definitive big-creator version of the friend-exposure game pires-potes digitizes.
**Rights.** Thumbnail + credit is low-risk; the real flag is the game's source — "Amis de merde" is a commercial board game (Olé! Studio), so never reuse its box art, card text or name. No French mega-creator version of this exact format was confirmed.
**Verification.** All confirmed, including Itoko & Co (FR). The TikTok link is a format-discover hub, not a single creator's clip — use a generic trend visual.

### mime-inverse
**Why it matches.** In Fallon's "Reverse Charades" the performer is verbally puppeteered: their partner tells them how to act out a secret clue, and the performer must guess it purely from the actions they perform. Recurring, instantly recognizable format (Anne Hathaway edition is the flagship).
**Rights.** Primary and alternates are network-TV clips (NBCUniversal; FR alternate is TF1's VTEP) — aggressive rights holders. Use only the platform-served thumbnail with clear credit and a link-out to the official channel URL; never re-host or re-edit.
**Verification.** All confirmed, including the official @fallontonight TikTok clip and TF1's "Vendredi Tout Est Permis" official channel.

### gauche-ou-droite
**Why it matches.** THE original viral source: Théo Delemazure's GPT-3 site (Oct 2022) declares any absurd item "de gauche" or "de droite" — it broke the French internet and spawned the exact debate mechanic (and name) of this game.
**Rights.** Primary is a website — use a screenshot of its UI with credit/link-out (low risk, individual creator). For TikTok alternates avoid showing identifiable street-interviewees' faces; since the format is politically themed, credit neutrally without implying creator endorsement of Bloop.
**Verification.** Site live (HTTP 200). TOMECO TikTok confirmed via oEmbed. X/Twitter launch-tweet URL reachable. The Aymeric Lompret interview is on **Le Nouvel Obs**'s channel (press media — text credit only, resolves the "chaîne non confirmée" flag).
